    $ rules per Bubenik section, iterated from a, b, etc onward alphabetically. 

$ ASSUMPTIONS / CONVENTIONS: 
$ /a/ treated as back. 
$ h3 is labial in some way
$ no laryngeals are anterior, all are obstruent
$ h2 and h3 are back, not front
$ h2 is not high, not labial in any way

$ writing i̯ and u̯ as j and w to save effort
